# Content source (from the LEAD Prelims course site, 13 Nov 2026)

Use for social posts, sales and marketing content only. Not for student data.

- `tests.json`: every free test on the site, with answer keys and explanations (1,155 questions incl. CSAT). `scripts/qbank.py` reads it.
- `items.json`, `notes.json`, `modules.json`, `depth.json`: what is free on the site, notes index, syllabus modules.
- `LEANPREP_PRELIMS_2027_BROCHURE_V4.pdf`, `LEANPREP_PRELIMS_2027_TEST_STRUCTURE_V10.pdf`: facts for posts (prices, test counts, Open Test dates).

When the site changes, replace these files with the new versions.
